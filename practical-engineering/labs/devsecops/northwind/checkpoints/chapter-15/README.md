# Chapter 15 checkpoint

This checkpoint proves a bounded recovery claim after Chapter 14 containment.

- **Inputs:** preserved incident evidence, contained operational state, inherited recovery interfaces, trust inventory, eradication plan, rebuild manifest, and verification criteria.
- **Mechanism:** trust-graph reconciliation, authority rotation, cache invalidation, release admission, runtime evaluation, detection replay, and consecutive business-health windows.
- **Outputs:** persistence replay, eradication decision, trusted rebuild, business reconciliation, and recovery verification records under `build/`.
- **Success means:** old modeled authority and artifacts fail, the source-to-runtime chain agrees on a new artifact, business state reconciles, and detections remain active for two consecutive windows.
- **Limitation:** the result covers the modeled inventory and collected evidence. It cannot prove that every possible persistence mechanism is absent.
