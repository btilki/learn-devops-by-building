# Chapter 3 checkpoint

The baseline proves that publishing 99.9 percent on Storefront alone, quoting a customer SLA as the target, and emitting remaining budget as a constant leaves `dispatch-fulfillment` without a window or computed budget.

The completed checkpoint verifies that every Chapter 1 journey has a numeric SLO, Fulfillment's target and window are not a copy of Storefront's, `notification-service` is non-critical, the customer SLA is out of scope, and remaining unreliability is computed from observations.

It cannot prove that 99.5 percent or 99.0 percent is the right commercial target, and it does not compute burn from a real telemetry backend.
