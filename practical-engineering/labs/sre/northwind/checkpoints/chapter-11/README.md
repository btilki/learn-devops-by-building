# Chapter 11 checkpoint

The baseline proves that a polished postmortem with a hortatory `be-more-careful` action, self-verification, no cascade follow-through, and no coverage of the platform-product incident treats a document as a reliability control.

The completed checkpoint verifies record-or-waiver coverage of both Chapter 10 incident ids, an owned dated cascade action verified from the Chapter 9 shed, independent verification, a toil-bound join, and forbidden hortatory labels.

It cannot prove that an organization actually learned, and it does not prove that 30 days is the right commercial review cadence.
