# Chapter 1 checkpoint

The completed checkpoint verifies declared owners, journeys, refusals, reciprocal ownership, and journey-completion proofs. It rejects `cluster-uptime` and platform job-time ids as success evidence.

It cannot prove that the chosen journeys are the right journeys for a real company.
