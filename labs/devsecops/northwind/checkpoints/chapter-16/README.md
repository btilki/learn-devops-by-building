# Chapter 16 checkpoint

This checkpoint proves that governance recomputes assurance from current operational evidence instead of trusting a green report.

- **Inputs:** owned control catalog, evidence map, review calendar, assurance report, live exceptions, telemetry, threat changes, custody evidence, and recovery verification.
- **Mechanism:** control/evidence graph resolution plus Chapter 11 policy, Chapter 13 detection, Chapter 14 custody, and Chapter 15 recovery evaluators.
- **Outputs:** assurance failure, reopened risk decision, corrected assurance report, and security improvement backlog under `build/`.
- **Success means:** every objective has an owner, implementation, independent evidence, limitation, and review trigger; stale or missing evidence fails assurance.
- **Limitation:** this local model supports assurance reasoning. It does not certify compliance with any legal or industry framework.
